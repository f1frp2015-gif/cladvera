# Singyes smart glass: facade-only supplier source review

Reviewed 11 October 2026. The user narrowed the website addition to smart glass that can be considered for facade panels. This record supersedes the earlier interior/retrofit recommendation. Cladvera's page and catalog entry should cover project-specific facade glazing inquiries only; interior partitions, healthcare privacy, self-adhesive retrofit film and automotive products are outside this addition.

## Publication conclusion

The official supplier site supports a facade inquiry offering: it identifies curtain-wall-specific dimming film and publishes a dedicated building-curtain-wall application section. The evidence does **not** establish a standard facade panel SKU, a universal glass build-up or project approval. Cladvera may present smart facade glazing for manufacturer and facade-team review, with the facade-suitable PDLC grade and complete glazing assembly confirmed for the project. Cladvera remains the supplier, not the manufacturer. [Current English homepage](https://www.syeamt.com/en), [curtain-wall applications](https://www.syeamt.com/en/case_63/)

Suggested scope wording: “Review switchable PDLC glazing for facade and curtain-wall projects. Confirm the manufacturer's facade-grade film, complete glazing construction, electrical controls and project evidence before specification.” This is an editorial recommendation, not a supplier quotation or a certification claim.

## Product form and optical description

A PDLC lamination film is a component of a glass construction. The curtain-wall-specific film promotion does not by itself define a finished laminated or insulating glass unit. Whether a proposed facade requires a particular laminate, insulating unit, coating, seal, frame or structural attachment must be confirmed by the manufacturer and project team.

The current English lamination-film page supports the basic transparent-on/frosted-off explanation. Its published off-state light transmission indicates a light-transmitting privacy state, not blackout. A diagram should be labeled as an illustration, with no claim that it predicts the appearance, solar behavior or privacy of an installed facade. [Lamination-film source](https://www.syeamt.com/en/products_59/76.html)

Do not describe this offering as electrochromic glazing, an opaque rainscreen panel or a ready-made replacement for any existing facade system. “Smart facade glazing” is the more precise catalog description.

## Specifications not transferable to the facade entry

The generic lamination-film page contains thickness, width, optical, electrical, temperature and service-life figures. They are film-level reference values; none was verified as the specification of the curtain-wall grade or a finished facade glass panel. Its response-time values omit a time unit. Its electrical table lists AC 50 Hz, which is not evidence that a supplied controller is suitable for a North American installation. [Film parameter source](https://www.syeamt.com/en/products_59/76.html)

The earlier [legacy laminated-glass page](https://en.syeamt.com/product/182.html) was available through search-index text, but direct access failed. Do not carry its dimensions, glass thickness notation or performance figures into a facade specification. The dedicated facade source above is the relevant basis for the new page's scope.

No offered-product safety-glazing, fire, impact, wind-load, air/water, acoustic, thermal, electrical or local-code approval was verified. Likewise, no price, stock, delivery date, warranty, energy-saving percentage or authorized-distributor status was established. These are open project inputs rather than negative conclusions about the manufacturer.

## Facade inquiry inputs

Request elevation drawings, pane dimensions and quantities, project location and exposure, design intent, proposed glazing build-up, coatings and insulating-unit requirements, frame and edge details, fixing/support conditions, electrical and controller locations, switching zones, maintenance access and destination. Ask the manufacturer and facade designer to identify the offered grade and assembly, applicable test evidence, installation requirements and sample/mock-up scope.

The website should use a facade application tag and an English manufacturer-source link. Keep film-only and retrofit products outside the catalog entry; mention film only as the internal switchable component. Generic film specifications and interior application imagery should not imply facade suitability.
