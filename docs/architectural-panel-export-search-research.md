# Architectural Panel Export Search Research

> **Consolidation note:** This report combines the reviewed product catalogue, existing keyword map, public search research and manufacturer, government and search-platform sources. Version v1.1, 9 October 2026. Update this document and its changelog instead of creating dated copies.
>
> **Purpose:** Help overseas architects, facade consultants, contractors, buyers and importers find Cladvera through material-selection and China-sourcing questions, then proceed to a request tied to a named product. GEO means generative engine optimization. Geographic targeting separately concerns United States and Canadian project inquiries. Website copy, keywords and this report use English.

## 1. Positioning and evidence limits

Cladvera's commercial entry point is architectural panel supply and China-sourcing coordination. Product facts remain specific to each manufacturer and construction. The catalogue contains 11 product families across six material/component categories. The China-sourcing theme covers ALMINE metal composite, Compactwood exterior HPL and interior decorative boards, and Kinflare custom GFRP. TAKTL is a separate US manufacturer reference and must not be classified as China-made.

Public search results helped identify terminology; manufacturer, association, government and official search-platform sources constrained the resulting content. This is **topic and intent research**, not a demand ranking exported from a paid keyword tool:

- Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, Keyword Planner, Ahrefs and Semrush datasets were not available.
- No search volume, keyword difficulty, CPC, existing organic position, traffic forecast or AI citation uplift is claimed.
- The CSV contains 166 entries: 142 search expressions and 24 question prompts. They are planning variants with unmeasured demand, not a claim that every exact phrase appeared in search suggestions.
- The 23 clusters are prioritized by product relevance, proximity to an inquiry, available evidence and content gaps. P0 means immediate implementation priority; P1 covers relevant product/technical needs; P2 covers narrower branded families. These are not demand-volume grades.
- Content covers the meaning of these queries. It does not repeat all 166 phrases verbatim or create a separate URL for every variation.

Full list: [Keyword and question CSV](seo-geo-keywords.csv). URL and technical rules: [Keyword map](seo-keyword-map.md).

## 4. Geographic and export positioning

The site addresses US and Canadian project inquiries. It does not create city doorway pages or invent local offices, warehouses, certifications or production. Country-specific copy needs real decision value rather than a changed place name.

The China-sourcing page separates manufacturer, production location, seller/exporter and dispatch point. TAKTL retains its separate source. Procurement connects product, evidence, shipment and receiving records to the quotation process.

Import content identifies information to prepare and verify. It does not assign HS codes, tariff rates, trade-remedy scope, DDP commitments or fixed lead times. Official sources support general importer responsibilities, composition and origin records, classification and charges, separate building-use review and wood-packaging checks: [CBP](https://www.help.cbp.gov/s/article/Article-1169?language=en_US), [CBSA product and origin information](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/import/guide-2-eng.html), [CBSA classification and charges](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/import/guide-3-eng.html), [NRC construction product approvals](https://nrc.canada.ca/en/certifications-evaluations-standards/canadian-construction-materials-centre/construction-product-approvals-canada), [ITA Incoterms](https://www.trade.gov/know-your-incoterms), [APHIS wood packaging](https://www.aphis.usda.gov/plant-imports/wood-packaging-material), [CFIA wood packaging](https://inspection.canada.ca/en/plant-health/forestry/imports/wood-packaging).

Geographic relevance does not establish Buy American, BABA or Buy Canadian eligibility; these procurement qualifications are outside the site's supply commitments.

## 5. GEO: align questions, answers and evidence

The update adds 28 visible questions: four on each of four material collections, six on the China-sourcing page and six in the material guide. They address supplier identity, China-sourcing scope, ACM/MCM, GFRP/GFRC, interior-board classification, A2 terminology, RFQ inputs and origin versus dispatch.

Implementation principles:

1. Answer the question first, then explain conditions and a useful next step.
2. Render definitions, tables and product names as server-side HTML text, without requiring an interaction to reveal essential information.
3. Distinguish the product, manufacturer and Cladvera's supplier role; avoid a catalogue-wide China-made label.
4. Generate FAQ schema from the same data as visible answers. Do not invent hidden answers, offers, reviews, inventory, certifications or author credentials.
5. Link product facts to manufacturer references and import questions to official institutions.
6. Use FAQs to help visitors, without promising Google FAQ rich results. Google announced that those results stopped appearing on 7 May 2026. [Google updates](https://developers.google.com/search/updates)

Google's official AI guidance emphasizes established SEO and useful content; special AI markup and llms.txt are not required. The existing llms.txt was corrected as an auxiliary directory, not treated as a Google ranking factor. [Google AI optimization guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide)

Microsoft recommends clear organization, direct answers and supporting evidence. Bing AI Performance can show cited pages and grounding expressions. Citation observations are not rankings, and publishing these pages does not establish an AI recommendation. [Microsoft content guidance](https://about.ads.microsoft.com/en/blog/post/october-2025/optimizing-your-content-for-inclusion-in-ai-search-answers), [Bing AI Performance](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/2026/2/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview/)

OAI-SearchBot and GPTBot have different purposes. The update does not change training-crawler policy; reviewed public content uses the existing common crawl rules. [OpenAI crawler documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots)

## 8. Verification and measurement after publication

Immediately testable: HTTP status, one H1 per page, metadata length, canonical/index directives, sitemap, parseable JSON-LD matching visible answers, links/anchors, responsive layout, menus and product-specific RFQ selection.

Platform data is needed to evaluate indexing, impressions, clicks, actual queries, AI citations and qualified inquiries. No search-platform account was created or accessed, and no outreach messages were sent in this update.

| Review window | Action | Evidence to use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| When platform access is available | Check the 23 indexable URLs and sitemap; inspect both new pages | Crawl/index status and selected canonical; do not treat a `site:` result count as a complete index report. |
| After sufficient impressions | Group queries and landing pages into the 23 clusters; separate brand/non-brand and US/Canada | Impressions, clicks, CTR and average position; avoid conclusions from small samples. |
| 30-day review | Improve titles/descriptions where impressions indicate a relevant opportunity; inspect the product-request path | Page-specific queries and conversion context; avoid changing every page at once. |
| 60–90-day review | Review Bing AI Performance and repeat the fixed 24 question prompts | Cited sources and factual correctness, including China/TAKTL distinctions; record date, platform, region and prompt. |
| After real inquiries arrive | Review product family, destination and document/sample/quote stage | Share of inquiries with identifiable project and quantity information; no new tracking script was added in this round. |

A confirmed domain, accurate legal seller and contact details, authentic project records and verified product reports are future evidence inputs. Existing search-platform verification environment variables can be used when available; missing business facts should remain unclaimed.
